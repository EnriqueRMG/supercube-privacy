1. Data controller and contact

The controller for SuperCube data is Enrique Ruiz-Medrano García, based in Spain. For privacy questions and requests concerning your data, email wallcreeperlab@gmail.com.

This policy covers the SuperCube game for Android, identified as com.wallcreeperlab.supercube. It explains how data is processed for the game, its online features and its advertising. Some earlier test versions may lack some of the options described here. You can still contact us by email.

2. Data the game uses

  • Local data includes audio, sensitivity, vibration and effects settings, personal records, statistics and recent run history, and scores queued for submission when the connection is restored.
  • Your game account includes a player ID generated by Unity Authentication, a nickname and technical session credentials. Using online features may automatically create a guest identity even if you neither enter an email address nor link Google.
  • Optional linking to Google Play Games uses identifiers and technical credentials that let the game recognise your account and recover your game identity. SuperCube does not request or receive your Google password. The current app does not request access to Gmail or its contents.
  • Online leaderboard data includes your player ID, nickname, score, position, level reached, best streak, duration and average angular accuracy. Historical records may include continues used. To verify scores, we also process run summaries such as hits, misses, number of encounters, health, rules, the run seed, and session identifiers and timings.
  • Online providers may process your IP address and the technical logs they need to provide and protect their services. A player ID can link sessions from the same account, so it does not make the data anonymous.
  • If you contact support by email, your email address, message and any information you provide are processed to respond to your request.

Current versions do not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location to play. The game has no active Spotify integration or access to a personal music library. The game has no additional analytics or attribution platform such as Firebase or AppsFlyer. The providers the game uses may still process technical data.

If you used a username and password in an earlier prototype, Unity Authentication may still hold that identity. You can also request deletion of those data using the contact address above.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Data is processed to provide the game and leaderboard features you use, maintain your identity and link it when you ask. The intended legal basis is performance of the service you request. Validation and security data help protect leaderboard integrity and prevent abuse, based on the legitimate interest in maintaining a reliable service. We process enquiries to handle your request and, where applicable, comply with legal obligations.

Where optional processing requires consent, we must request it before that processing begins. Playing the game or reading this policy does not constitute consent to personalised advertising. You can exercise the rights described below, including the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

4. Information visible to other players

Online leaderboards display your nickname, score and position. Leaderboard services may also expose your player ID and information associated with your score, such as level, streak, duration and continues. Avoid using your real name, email address or other personal details as your nickname if you want to keep them private.

Score validation may prevent a score from being posted if it does not meet the service rules. You can contact us to request a review. These checks concern eligibility for the leaderboard and do not amount to automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Providers and recipients

SuperCube uses Unity Gaming Services, including Authentication, Leaderboards, Cloud Code and Cloud Save, for player identity, leaderboards and run validation. Unity processes data to provide those services and, in the cases described in its own terms, for its independent purposes. See the Unity privacy policy for game players and app users.

If you link Google Play Games, Google helps authenticate you and processes data for its services under the Google Privacy Policy. We handle enquiries sent to our contact address through Gmail. Linking your account does not give other players access to your Google Account.

We may also disclose necessary data to comply with a legal obligation or a valid request from an authority. We do not provide other players with your access credentials or session tokens.

GitHub Pages hosts the privacy and deletion pages. GitHub may process technical data about your visit, including your IP address, to provide and protect its hosting service. These pages do not use analytics, submission forms, remote fonts or their own tracking technology. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

6. Advertising

SuperCube includes advertising and does not offer a general ad-free mode. Non-rewarded interstitial ads may appear between runs in Arcade and Adventure without interrupting play. The first run after opening the game starts without an interstitial. Later transitions may show one after more than two minutes of active play have accumulated. Pauses, menus, ads and time in the background do not count towards that time. You can continue playing if no ad is available or it fails to display.

Adventure may display a banner at the top, including during pauses and upgrade choices. Practice may display a banner at the bottom during play and hide it when paused. Ads are spaced away from controls and respect the device's safe area. You can also choose to watch a rewarded ad to earn Cubes, up to once per UTC day according to the server. Watching this ad is optional and does not let you continue after defeat.

Advertising information and this policy are available under Settings → Privacy & Account. Earlier local preferences for enabling or disabling ads are retained for compatibility. They no longer control whether ads appear and do not signify consent to personalisation.

The integration uses Unity LevelPlay and the ironSource Ads and Chartboost networks, depending on the version and available ad inventory. Depending on the applicable features and choices, these providers may process advertising or device identifiers, IP addresses, technical information and ad interactions to serve and measure ads and detect fraud. See the Unity Privacy Policy linked above and the Chartboost and LoopMe Privacy Policy.

This integration requests ads without consent for personalisation and with an opt-out from the sale of data. Playing or viewing ads is not treated as consent to personalisation. Data processing may still take place under these restrictions, and any applicable additional consent requirements still apply. If we add new networks or purposes that require a separate choice, we will update the information before enabling them.

Some earlier test versions contain an in-game ad simulation. That simulation is not advertising served by an ad network.

7. Data retention

Settings and personal records stay on the device until you delete them using your system settings or they are replaced. The local test log keeps the twenty most recent run files. Deleting local app data does not by itself delete an account or its online scores.

Online identity and leaderboard data are retained to maintain the account and leaderboards until they are deleted or removed in line with service needs and applicable requests. The version reviewed does not automatically delete inactive accounts. Older leaderboards may remain stored after the rules change. The protected validation record for a run is replaced when a new run for that account is recorded.

Support requests are retained as long as necessary to handle them and, where applicable, to meet obligations or defend claims. If a specific legal reason requires keeping data after a deletion request, we will explain the reason and the applicable retention period or criterion. We do not promise a deletion date for backups outside the controller's control without checking with the provider.

8. International processing and security

Services from Unity, Google and Chartboost/LoopMe may involve processing outside the European Economic Area. Safeguards depend on the service and country and include mechanisms provided in the applicable terms, such as standard contractual clauses where applicable. You can request more information on the applicable safeguards. See Unity's Data Processing Addendum and the Google and Chartboost policies linked above.

Access to online services uses the providers' connections and authentication mechanisms. Keep technical credentials private. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Where applicable, you can request access to, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Email wallcreeperlab@gmail.com with your request and the minimum information needed to locate your account.

We respond within the applicable legal time limits, generally within one month for GDPR requests, subject to any extensions allowed by law. If there are reasonable doubts about the requester's identity, we will ask for proportionate verification. Do not send passwords or access codes. You can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency or the competent data protection authority.

10. Request account and data deletion

You can request deletion by emailing wallcreeperlab@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my SuperCube account”. Include your full nickname and your player ID if you know it. If you cannot access the app, explain that in your email. You do not need to reinstall it to make the request. We verify that you own the account before making irreversible changes. A public nickname alone does not prove ownership.

The request covers your SuperCube identity and the associated data managed by the controller, including leaderboard scores and validation data, subject to any applicable legal retention exceptions. Deleting your SuperCube account does not delete your Google Account. Uninstalling the game or unlinking an account does not request deletion of online data.

In versions that offer this option, you can start your request from Settings → Privacy & Account → Request Account Deletion or from Leaderboards → Account → Privacy & Account. The game prepares an email for you to review and send. We handle requests manually. Opening the email or this page does not send a request automatically or confirm that data has been deleted. If your version does not include this option, email us directly.

11. Children

SuperCube is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child's data has been processed in a way that required authorisation that was not obtained, please contact us so we can review the case and take appropriate action. A general audience rating does not replace requirements that apply by age or territory.

12. Changes to this policy

This page shows when it was last updated. If we add features or providers that change how data is processed, we will update the information and ask for any choices or consent needed before starting the processing that requires them.
