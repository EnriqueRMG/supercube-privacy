Delete your account and data

Last updated 16 November 2026

You can ask Enrique Ruiz-Medrano García to delete your SuperCube account and associated data by emailing wallcreeperlab@gmail.com.

How to make a request

  1. Use the subject Delete my SuperCube account.
  2. Include your full nickname and your player ID if you know it. Explain if you have lost access to the game. You can make a request without reinstalling the game or signing in on this page.
  3. We verify account ownership before deleting data. A public nickname alone does not prove ownership. Do not send passwords, Google verification codes or identity documents unless asked to provide appropriate proof.
Prepare a deletion email

This opens a draft in your email app for you to review and send. Selecting this link does not send the message or delete any data. If your email app does not open, write to the address above.

What the request covers

Your SuperCube identity, related scores on its leaderboards and validation data managed by the controller, subject to any applicable legal retention exceptions that we explain to you.

This does not delete your Google Account. Uninstalling the game or clearing its local data does not by itself delete online data. We also check any earlier services and leaderboards associated with your account.

Response and retention

We respond within the applicable GDPR time limits, generally within one month, subject to any extensions allowed by law. If there is a legal reason to keep any data, we will explain the reason and the applicable retention period or criterion.

Requesting deletion from the game

In versions that offer this option, open Settings → Privacy & Account → Request Account Deletion or Leaderboards → Account → Privacy & Account. The game prepares an email for you to review and send. If your version does not include this option, email us directly at the address above.

We handle requests manually after verifying account ownership. This page has no submission form and does not delete data automatically.
